Bioclimatic variables from ERA5-Land

We computed the 19 WorldClim-standard bioclimatic indices from ERA5-Land reanalysis (hourly, 0.1°, global). Four are temperature-based, the rest precipitation-based. Each variable is available for every year from 1980 to 2020 as a compressed, tiled GeoTIFF.
